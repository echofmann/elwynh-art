Custom Commissions

Architectural sketches and detailed drawings tailored to your vision. From building portraits to urban studies, each piece is created specifically for you.

Building Portraits

Detailed architectural drawings of specific buildings, homes, or structures. Perfect for commemorating a special place or capturing architectural heritage.

  • Site visit and initial sketches
  • Detailed pen and ink drawing
  • Multiple revisions included
  • High-quality paper and archival inks

$300 - $500

Urban Sketches

Field sketch style drawings of cityscapes, streetscapes, or neighborhood views. Captures the energy and character of urban environments.

  • On-location sketching session
  • 1-3 hour field sketch style
  • Immediate, gestural quality
  • Perfect for smaller spaces

$200 - $350

Interior Studies

Architectural interior drawings focusing on space, light, and design details. Ideal for capturing the atmosphere of meaningful interior spaces.

  • Interior space documentation
  • Light and shadow emphasis
  • Furniture and detail inclusion
  • Multiple perspective options

$250 - $400

Commission Process

1

Initial Consultation

Submit your inquiry with reference photos and vision. We'll discuss your project goals and technical requirements.

2

Quote & Agreement

Receive detailed quote and timeline (usually 1-3 weeks). 50% deposit secures your spot in the schedule.

3

Creation Process

Site visit and initial sketches, followed by detailed studio work. Regular progress updates throughout.

4

Completion & Delivery

Final approval, remaining payment, and secure delivery or pickup of your finished artwork.
