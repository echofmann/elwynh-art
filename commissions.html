Commissions

Architectural drawings tailored to your vision. From building portraits to urban studies, each piece is created specifically for you. All commissioned drawings are professionally framed and include archival matboard.

Commission Process

1

Initial Consultation

Submit an inquiry with your project details and reference photos. Standard sizes receive immediate pricing; custom dimensions include a brief discussion to prepare an accurate quote.

2

Agreement & Deposit

You'll receive a finalized quote and an estimated timeline (typically 3–4 weeks). A 50% deposit confirms the commission and reserves your place in the studio schedule.

3

Creation

Work begins using your approved reference materials. The piece is completed in the studio over the agreed timeline, allowing for a focused creative process.

4

Completion & Delivery

Once the artwork is finished, the remaining balance is due. Your framed piece is then professionally packaged and shipped securely to your address.
